Roomful turns any website into a room: you see everyone's cursors, chat next to them, react and draw on a shared board. With two lines of code.

02The / key

Talk where you're looking.

Press / (or tap ⌨ on the pill) and type: your message appears next to your cursor, live, for the whole room. Enter keeps it for a few seconds and Esc clears it.

03The E key

Applaud without saying a word.

Press E wherever your cursor is and pick an emoji (on a phone, tap ⌨ on the pill). They float up from there and the whole room sees them. And to say hi, just shake your cursor from side to side: 👋.

Ghost mode

Tap the 👻 on the pill and walk through the room unseen: other cursors disappear and yours stops being shared. You disconnect completely: nobody sees you, not even in the list.

Walked into an empty room? You won't be alone.

When nobody's around, a couple of regulars wander in, look around and wave hello now and then. They wear a BOT label, so nobody mistakes them for people. When real people arrive, most of them head for the door, but one always stays to keep you company.

06Leave your mark

A wall for everyone in the room.

Draw or write whatever you like: it stays here for whoever comes next. Your ink refills every 3 hours, so make it count. When the board fills up, the oldest drawings fade and get erased first, so there's always something to see. Hover over a drawing to see who made it.

07For developers

Two lines and your site is a roomful.

<!-- 1. The script -->
<script src="/roomful.js"></script>

<!-- 2. Connect it to your server -->
<script>
  Roomful.init({
    url: "wss://cursors.yoursite.com/ws",
    lang: "en",
    bots: 2,
    onStats: s => console.log(s.page, "people here")
  });
</script>
