Someone else is reading this.

Right now, everyone on this page is in the room with you. You meet them through one small piece: the pill in the bottom-right corner, your door to the room. It has four parts; here's each one, and every demo is real.

01 — People
1 The faces on the left

Who else is in the room?

The faces and the number tell you how full the room is. Click them to see who each person is, whether they're active, typing or stepped away, and a button to follow them: you'll go wherever they go.

The numbers come from the same source as the pill (onStats), so you can show them anywhere on your site.

2 The speech bubble

Talk where you're looking.

Click the speech bubble or press / and type: your message appears next to your cursor, live, for the whole room. Enter keeps it for a few seconds and Esc clears it.

03 — Reactions
3 The smiley

Applaud without saying a word.

The smiley opens the emojis. With the keyboard it's faster: press E wherever your cursor is and pick one. They float up from there and the whole room sees them.

Try it right here: click any of these emojis.

Integrate
</> For developers

Two lines and your site is a roomful.

<!-- 1. The script -->
<script src="/roomful.js"></script>

<!-- 2. Connect it to your server -->
<script>
  Roomful.init({
    url: "wss://cursors.yoursite.com/ws",
    lang: "en",
    bots: 2,
    onStats: s => console.log(s.page, "people here")
  });
</script>
